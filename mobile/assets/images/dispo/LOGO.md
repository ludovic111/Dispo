# Dispo logo — 9 September 2026

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG for the app icon. The same asset is used for the in-app mark and splash screen. No paid external image API or new dependency was added.

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final square iOS app icon for Dispo, a refined Swiss music collaboration app. One extremely simple, distinctive lowercase d monogram fused into a musical note: a rounded hollow notehead forming the bowl of the d, a single straight ascending stem on its right, with a short elegantly curved musical flag at the top. The silhouette must read both as lowercase d and a single musical note. Bold balanced geometric curves, clean flat vector-like edges, restrained Swiss modern design, legible at 32px. Exactly one centered symbol occupying about 60% of canvas width and height with ample equal safe margins. Color palette strictly from existing app: full-bleed solid midnight navy #050814 background; symbol predominantly jazz blue #0099FF with a very subtle transition to cyan #00D2FF near the top. No shadows, no glow, no texture, no bevel, no 3D, no white, no decorative shapes, no staff lines, no other notes, no wordmark or text. Full square 1024x1024 opaque production icon, no rounded outer corners, no device mockup, no border, no presentation sheet.
