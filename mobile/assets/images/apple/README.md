The API grants conditional promotional use of album artwork, not ownership or unrestricted reuse:
https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api (Legal)

The app displays Apple CDN artwork alongside an official store badge linking to that recording. Both use the same eligibility function; absent an official image or direct Apple link, the image is replaced with a generic note. The badges have at least 30 points height and surrounding clear space. The app does not play or download the audio previews.

Do not use catalogue artwork to advertise Dispo, in its app icon, or in marketing screenshots. Honour Apple takedown requests and forward received claims to Apple. A CDN hostname is a technical allowlist, not an independent licence: retain the existing iTunes search / Apple catalogue source chain when changing providers.
