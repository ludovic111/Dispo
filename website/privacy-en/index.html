Privacy
policy.

This policy explains which data Dispo processes, why it is used and the choices available to you.

ControllerDataUseSharingYour rights

1. Data controller

Dispo is published by Raphaël Herrera, Geneva, Switzerland. For any privacy question, contact support@dispo.ch.

2. Data we process

Depending on the features you use, Dispo processes:

  • identity and contact data: name, handle and email address;
  • musician profile data: instruments, genres, experience, repertoire, availability, city and postal code;
  • content you provide: profile photo, demo videos, SOS requests, messages, groups and documents;
  • social data: follows, favorites, collaborations, blocks and reports;
  • technical identifiers required for accounts, security and service operation;
  • purchase information supplied by Apple to establish Premium entitlements. Dispo does not receive payment card details.

Dispo does not collect precise GPS location in the current version and does not use data for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. How we use data

We use data to create and secure accounts, display profiles, match gig requests with available musicians, enable communication, provide Premium features, answer support requests and moderate content. Depending on the activity, processing is based on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in protecting the platform, legal obligations or your consent.

4. Hosting and recipients

Application data is hosted by Supabase in Europe, in the Zurich region according to the project configuration. Apple processes in-app purchases under its own terms. We do not sell personal data. Data may be disclosed to providers required to operate the service, authorities where legally required, or as part of a legal transaction involving Dispo.

Public visibility

Information published in profiles or gig requests is visible to other users according to app settings and features. Private messages are available only to participants and authorized persons where required for safety or support.

5. Retention and security

Data is kept while your account is active, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed, subject to legal retention periods, abuse prevention and dispute resolution. Server-side access controls and security rules restrict access to data.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to certain processing. You can edit many details in the app and delete your account from Profile → My account.

To exercise a right, email support@dispo.ch. We may request proof of identity where necessary. You may also contact your competent data protection authority.

7. Children and changes

Dispo is not intended for anyone below the age required to validly consent to data processing in their country. This policy may change as the service evolves; the date at the top shows the latest update.
